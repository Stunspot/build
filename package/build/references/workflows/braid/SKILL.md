# BRAID

Turn one user objective into three separately addressable, cooperating Codex tasks: **MAKER MAKES. CHECKER CHECKS. SUPERVISOR SUPERVISES.** Each has one governing responsibility. Let the actual work determine everything that responsibility requires, through to the user's intended result and an applicable independent verdict.

Recognize **"Create a BRAID to do [TASKNAME]: [task explanation]"**, explicit **$braid** invocation, and requests to resume an existing BRAID. Preserve the complete explanation and later corrections. Discussing, preparing, or adjusting this skill ends at that requested state.

## Enter through the objective

Read pertinent project instructions, existing work, and the live skill/tool inventory. Resolve outcome, scope, constraints, existing authority, and meaningful evidence of success. Preserve effective personas, promptcraft, architecture, and valuable behavior. Ask only for missing information that changes a consequential decision; progress around ordinary uncertainty.

Search project records and the native task inventory for this objective before creating tasks. Recover real identifiers and next actions on repeated invocation. Compare objectives and project context, rather than titles alone. Recover missing strands without duplicating survivors; resolve uncertain creation outcomes through native lookup before retry.

Assign responsibility before choosing methods. MAKER does whatever making the requested result calls for. CHECKER independently determines whether the work deserves acceptance and does whatever checking it calls for. SUPERVISOR understands and directs the whole effort, doing whatever effective supervision calls for. Each preserves the caller's assistant identity and full available repertoire; responsibility determines how that competence is applied. BRAID itself requires no other skill or persona package. Select specialist skills only when they are available and useful; use appropriate available methods when they are absent. Derive the necessary actions from the objective, artifact and situation, rather than treating examples or named specialties as the role's job description.

Keep the division strict. MAKER owns changes to the deliverable; CHECKER owns independent judgment of it; SUPERVISOR owns the direction of the effort. Ordinary self-checking belongs to competent making, and inspecting work belongs to supervision, while neither replaces CHECKER's independent judgment. MAKER does not run TestForge; when it is available and applicable, it is normally CHECKER's first choice for technical checking. The harsh copy editor is another expression of checking. These illustrate the governing responsibility; they do not define its boundaries.

## Establish the three strands

Before launch read [the shared agreement](references/shared-agreement.md), the complete [MAKER](references/maker.md), [CHECKER](references/checker.md), and [SUPERVISOR](references/supervisor.md) directives, and [native coordination](references/native-coordination.md). The single-responsibility division in this skill governs all examples and methods. Use CodeFarm to understand the productive separation and interaction of making, checking and supervision. Its software personas illustrate the pattern; BRAID generalizes those responsibilities to the actual objective and uses real separate tasks.

Create exactly three distinct tasks:

| Task title | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| [TASKNAME] MAKER | MAKE the requested result |
| [TASKNAME] CHECKER | CHECK whether the work deserves acceptance |
| [TASKNAME] SUPERVISOR | SUPERVISE the whole effort toward the intended result |

The invocation chat is the launcher, not a fourth strand. Prefer persistent native Codex chats. Use separately addressable subagents only as the best available fallback; disclose their lifecycle limits and preserve three executable briefs. If host concurrency is limited, retain these three roles while executing them sequentially. Additional workers require user direction.

Maintain one compact durable work record in an accessible existing project location, adapting [the record template](assets/work-record.md). SUPERVISOR becomes its sole writer after launcher bootstrap. Each strand owns its output artifacts and sends consequential updates to SUPERVISOR.

Compose complete prompts from [the brief template](assets/task-brief.md): original human objective verbatim, relevant project instructions and facts, criteria and authority, shared paths, the full shared agreement and specialized role directive, selected resources, opening assignment, sibling references, and concrete messaging/resumption routes. Include the explicit human-originating communication grant inline in every prompt. Include the complete [heartbeat prompt](assets/supervisor-heartbeat.md), filled with real paths and IDs, in SUPERVISOR's brief once its roster is known. Paths supplement complete assignments.

Create and register real task IDs promptly. During bootstrap use exact titles and pending roster entries; begin useful discovery and proposal work while reserving production writes until ownership and substantive design agreement are resolved. Deliver the full three-ID roster, hosts, access arrangement, and originating authority to every strand. Transfer record ownership, establish the SUPERVISOR heartbeat through the actual automation tool, and verify first acknowledgments or exchanges. Partial registration remains CREATED.

## Make the collaboration live

The human's BRAID invocation authorizes all three participant chats to initiate and answer objective-related messages, critique, assignments, results, nudges, and resumptions among themselves, and to establish and maintain SUPERVISOR's heartbeat. Carry that originating human authority into every brief and roster dispatch. **Do not ask again whether these participants may talk.** This grant comes from the human's invocation and standing BRAID direction, rather than a sibling's request. Preserve narrower user constraints and actual tool rules; unrelated recipients and materially new authority remain outside it.

Assign MAKER a concrete design proposal and CHECKER an independent critique and improved alternative. SUPERVISOR frames the decision and ensures their discussion reaches an actionable agreement before substantive production. Both workers acknowledge role, objective, record access, workspace, opening work, and handoff route through an observed response or owned artifact.

Use separate workspaces/branches when warranted and authorized, or controlled writes with MAKER as production writer and CHECKER evaluating an identifiable snapshot. Confirm actual artifact visibility across worktrees or hosts. CHECKER derives expectations from the objective and retains its own findings and verdict.

| Collaboration state | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| PREPARED | Three complete executable briefs; no real task set |
| CREATED | Real tasks exist; activation or coordination is unverified |
| ACTIVE | Three real IDs, opening assignments, observed shared access and first exchanges, workable handoff/resumption route |

Track delivery, independent verdict, and heartbeat state separately. ACTIVE does not prove unattended supervision. Record the automation's real ID, target, cadence and observed enabled state; disclose unavailable scheduling without claiming continuous autonomy or blocking useful authorized work.

## Debate, make, challenge, repair

Before effectuating a substantive design, have MAKER and CHECKER vigorously discuss it. MAKER proposes concrete behavior and tradeoffs; CHECKER exposes weaknesses and offers substantive improvements; MAKER defends with evidence or revises; each reply advances the decision. Stop when both can state the chosen design, why it serves the objective, how material objections were resolved, and what uncertainty the smallest useful experiment will settle. For a small settled change, a concise substantive exchange suffices. Reopen discussion when discoveries materially alter the design. SUPERVISOR resolves stalled disagreement using purpose, evidence, or a discriminating probe; never equate forced agreement with quality.

Workers initiate handoffs as soon as their result changes the next action. MAKER normally sends candidates directly to CHECKER and reports them to SUPERVISOR; CHECKER sends findings directly to MAKER and reports its verdict to SUPERVISOR. Either may bring a proposal, blocker, discovery, decision request, or completion directly to whichever sibling needs it. Send the result and a concrete next action through the native wakeup tool before ending the turn. A saved artifact or finished turn alone never wakes the next owner. Continue available authorized work instead of waiting for SUPERVISOR to notice.

CHECKER checks the work as a whole against what the user actually needs. Discover what could make this result inadequate, choose the appropriate expertise and methods, and pursue those questions to an independent judgment. TestForge and the harsh copy editor illustrate checking in different situations. Neither defines its scope, and using both is no formula for acceptance. **"This is crap; do it again"** is appropriate when supported by a concrete failure, its consequence, and a substantive direction for improvement. Every checking activity must serve a useful judgment; stop expanding evidence once the intended outcome and applicable criteria are adequately established.

Bind candidate handoffs and **ACCEPTED / CHANGES REQUIRED / BLOCKED** verdicts to the exact examined revision, completed and omitted evaluations, material findings and residual risks. MAKER repairs; CHECKER reassesses affected behavior and nearby credible risks. Inspect underlying artifacts whenever judgment depends on a report.

## Supervise and finish

Create one heartbeat attached to the real SUPERVISOR chat, normally every ten minutes, using [native coordination](references/native-coordination.md) and the filled [heartbeat prompt](assets/supervisor-heartbeat.md). Direct messages drive immediate work between checks. Reuse the matching heartbeat on re-entry. Keep the prompt quiet on unchanged/non-actionable state; notify only for meaningful changes, completion, consequential failure, or required human action.

On every heartbeat SUPERVISOR examines both workers' current activity and decision-relevant artifacts, compares work with the intended result, and nudges the responsible strand when needed. Recognize and correct whatever is undermining the effort. Idle handoffs, role violations, circular debate and test-suite kabuki illustrate drift; examine the whole situation rather than monitoring only those symptoms. Intervene with the specific drift, a useful next action, and its observable stopping condition. Two consecutive checks without material progress require a method reassessment, not an automatic quality waiver or pause.

At interruption preserve the next actor, concrete action, and actual restart route. Retire the heartbeat at verified completion or owner cancellation; honor explicit owner pauses. Native scheduler absence is a stated limitation, not a reason to simulate autonomy.

Complete when the intended outcome and required deliverables exist, the integrated delivered revision matches CHECKER's applicable verdict, and material subsequent changes have been reassessed. User-authorized residual risk remains distinct from CHECKER acceptance.

At setup report the installed location and observed validation. At launch report the three actual task references, collaboration state, shared record, opening assignments, and heartbeat state. At completion present usable artifacts, independent verdict, and material residual risks. Let SUPERVISOR consolidate useful progress communication.

Keep the caller's assistant identity vivid in collaboration and backstage in machine-consumed artifacts.
