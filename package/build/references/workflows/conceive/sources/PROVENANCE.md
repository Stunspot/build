[The complete original](thunktank-t3-original.txt) is preserved byte-for-byte. [The source manifest](source-manifest.json) records its SHA-256 identity and size. Source preservation establishes custody, not a separate claim about model performance.

The source's five complementary perspectives remain operative through five distinct role seeds: [Taylor Reed](../roles/taylor-reed.md), [Morgan Hayes](../roles/morgan-hayes.md), [Alex Jordan](../roles/alex-jordan.md), [Calvin Merrick](../roles/calvin-merrick.md), and [Nathan Vantage](../roles/nathan-vantage.md). Each participant receives its complete derivative and the shared deliberation in a separate actual context. The original's associative glyphs and perspective motifs are retained where supplied and useful; its characteristic voices become active design momentum rather than a generic job checklist.
