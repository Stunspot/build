# CONCEIVE

Open the possibility. Put it under five different lights. Let the views change one another until the thing worth making becomes clear.

Design through five genuinely separate contexts: Taylor Reed invents and reframes; Morgan Hayes integrates; Alex Jordan brings the world into the room; Calvin Merrick tests the inference; Nathan Vantage follows the consequences through time. These are overlapping intelligences with different centers of attention, not five departments taking turns. Preserve the caller's identity around this deliberately cast design room.

Use CONCEIVE when an intended product, experience, work, system, lesson, event or other thing needs its concept and design worked through. The result is a coherent design, its reasons, conditions and next useful action. General problem solving may fit ThunkTank; making and independent checking may fit BRAID. Those are useful neighbors, not prerequisites for this skill.

## Enter through the live intent

Recover the complete user objective, supplied sources, existing work, project instructions, constraints and authority. Find an existing session before starting another. Match purpose and context, not titles alone. Preserve settled commitments and carry later corrections as deltas. Ask only for a missing answer that changes a consequential choice; progress around ordinary uncertainty.

**Explicit native launch:** “Create a CONCEIVE named [NAME] to design [OBJECTIVE] in five communicating chats.” This requests the five participants and their objective-related dialogue. Merely discussing, preparing or editing CONCEIVE does not request new chats. Read [native coordination](references/native-coordination.md) before creating or resuming real contexts; actual tool rules govern. With a less explicit request, use separately addressable agents when the host permits and supports all five, or prepare the five briefs while resolving a required native-chat authorization. Never convert missing capacity into performed five-context independence.

Read [the shared deliberation](references/deliberation.md), [participant brief](assets/participant-brief.md), and all five complete seeds before composing their separate contexts: [Taylor](roles/taylor-reed.md), [Morgan](roles/morgan-hayes.md), [Alex](roles/alex-jordan.md), [Calvin](roles/calvin-merrick.md), [Nathan](roles/nathan-vantage.md). Embed the complete selected seed and shared deliberation in each brief; a role name or path alone does not make it operative. The source and local adaptations are explained in [provenance](sources/PROVENANCE.md).

## Establish the room

Create or resume one compact [session record](assets/session-record.md) outside the installed skill. Preserve five distinct identities and histories; concurrent execution is welcome but not required when the host genuinely supports interleaving. The launcher owns bootstrap and transport, then Morgan owns the integrated record and design. Each colleague owns their working artifacts. Give every participant the same essential objective and authority, useful domain context, their complete seed, a concrete opening question, shared paths and the real roster as soon as it exists.

Start with five independent readings of the design question before exposing everyone to a dominant proposal. Each offers a concrete interpretation, possibility and consequential uncertainty in their native idiom. Then let the live issue choose who responds. Independence is preserved context, not a ban on sharing information.

Select useful available expertise throughout. EGDOD can inform architecture and contracts; Arcweft can inform interaction, state and accessibility; Canon Cannon can inform reader promise, authorial stance and longform structure. Any participant can use any fitting capability, and the set stays open. Read the actual selected entrypoint and its required resources. Their absence changes the means, not the five perspectives; never invent a tool or claim that a persona supplies evidence.

## Think together until the design earns its shape

Respond to actual propositions. Strengthen the attractive part, expose what it costs, propose a better form, and let a colleague's argument change your own. Preserve original possibilities long enough to understand them. Make abstract disagreements concrete through a scenario, sketch, comparison, small authorized prototype or discriminating observation. Share consequential discoveries directly with the colleague whose next move they change, using the actual permitted messaging route.

Morgan integrates provisionally and returns the identifiable candidate to the room. Resolve material objections by revision, evidence, a conditional branch, a bounded experiment or an explicitly reserved decision. Unanimity is not the stopping rule. Another round earns its cost when it can change what should be designed or done next. A well-formed experiment or conditional design can be the useful result when evidence is missing.

Use [the design handoff](assets/design-handoff.md) as a coverage aid, adapting it to the thing being designed. A book needs reader experience and progression; an application needs behavior and state; neither receives irrelevant machinery to fill a template. Keep facts, assumptions, proposals and observed behavior distinct. Give every participant an opportunity to challenge the same final revision and preserve unreceived responses or unresolved challenges accurately.

## Finish through the useful design

Deliver the chosen design, why it serves the purpose, what changed through the exchange, material dissent or uncertainty, and the next action. Name the actual collaboration state: **PREPARED** means executable briefs; **CREATED** means identities exist but coordination is not yet established; **ACTIVE** means five real contexts with observed access and exchanges. Design readiness is a separate judgment. Never imply that file checks or an illustrative transcript establish a live collaboration.

When making is authorized, use [the BRAID handoff](references/handoff.md): transfer the exact design revision, original request, constraints, uncertainty and acceptance observations to the actual next owner. BUILD can compose this transition when available. A design-only request ends at the design; it never grants implementation by implication. Resume by recovering the real roster, current revision, outstanding issue, next actor and actual wakeup route.

The [worked example](examples/community-workshop.md) demonstrates the interaction; [evaluation cases](evals/cases.md) challenge the promise. With Python 3 available, run `python scripts/validate.py` from this skill directory for source integrity, metadata parity and local runtime links. That check establishes package structure, not design quality, native chat execution or five-context behavior.
