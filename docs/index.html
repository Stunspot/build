04 / Stay in control

More context.
Real limits.

Set the boundary before launch.

Multiple contexts and recurring supervision use your host's time and model allowance. BUILD includes no model quota and promises no fixed cost. State a budget, choose only the workflow you need, and exclude unattended supervision when it does not fit.

Bring the right context.

Provide the outcome, users, constraints and existing decisions. Relevant material passes to collaborators. Keep secrets out of briefs; your host and connected tools govern data handling.

Finish means the thing is usable.

Prepared briefs, created chats and active collaboration are different states. Completion means the intended result exists, its delivered revision matches the applicable independent verdict, and remaining limits are explicit.
