# BUILD

![BUILD — Idea to finished work. Five design studies become three intertwined material strands and a finished pavilion.](docs/assets/readme-banner.png)

**🏗️ Idea to finished work: CONCEIVE designs; BRAID makes and checks.**

Good ideas lose things on the way to becoming real: the reason for a choice, the awkward constraint, the question nobody answered. BUILD carries those things forward.

It joins **CONCEIVE**, a design conversation across five separate contexts, with **BRAID**, three collaborators responsible for making, independent checking and supervision. The handoff carries your purpose, decisions, constraints and definition of success. If making exposes a design problem, the evidence returns to the relevant design question while useful work survives.

[Explore BUILD](https://stunspot.github.io/build/) · [Download v0.1.0 free](https://github.com/Stunspot/build/releases/latest/download/BUILD-v0.1.0.zip) · [Install and start](docs/start.md)

## Choose the part you need

| Your starting point | Use | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An idea you want designed and made | **BUILD** | A design carried into execution, with independent checking |
| A concept that needs working through | **CONCEIVE** | A coherent design, its reasons, unresolved conditions and next action |
| A settled objective ready to make | **BRAID** | Making, checking and supervision in three separate roles |

CONCEIVE and BRAID are complete bundled workflows and can be requested directly. BUILD can also take a settled design straight into BRAID. This is useful for a product, lesson, publication, event, tool or other concrete outcome; available tools determine what your host can actually do.

## Get to first use

Download the release ZIP, extract it, and keep the complete `build` folder together. Follow [Install and start](docs/start.md) for Codex installation, a discovery check and a small first task. Codex is the targeted host for v0.1.0; attaching the ZIP to a general chat supports only the session use that chat can provide.

Before you use the full launch command, know what it requests: **five communicating design chats, followed when ready by three execution chats, including one recurring SUPERVISOR check normally every ten minutes until completion.** Your narrower instructions still govern. The host supplies chat creation, communication and scheduling; a missing feature is reported, not simulated.

```text
Create a BUILD called Vocabulary Lab: design and make a small offline vocabulary practice tool. It should accept an editable word list, work with a keyboard, and let the learner stop at any time. Keep the first version small. Do not publish it or use paid services.
```

For a first look that creates no collaborators, use:

```text
Use BUILD to explain how you would approach this idea: [your idea]. Do not create chats, schedules or files yet.
```

You should first see the understood outcome, available collaboration mode and next action. A prepared brief is not a running collaborator. At completion, expect a usable artifact, the independent verdict for the delivered revision and any material limits.

## How the work holds together

CONCEIVE begins with five distinct readings: Taylor Reed explores possibilities, Morgan Hayes integrates, Alex Jordan brings practical context, Calvin Merrick tests the inference, and Nathan Vantage follows consequences through time. They challenge and improve the same developing design.

BRAID assigns responsibilities, not job titles: **MAKER makes. CHECKER checks. SUPERVISOR supervises.** The work can use fitting specialist skills if available; BUILD does not bundle a model, extra tools or those optional specialties.

## Cost, privacy and control

Multiple contexts and recurring supervision use your host's model allowance and may consume paid resources under your account. BUILD includes no model quota. State a time or usage budget, use CONCEIVE or BRAID alone when that fits, and add `Do not schedule unattended supervision` if you want to exclude the recurring check. Never assume a fixed cost per BUILD.

Share only material appropriate for your AI host and its connected tools. The workflow passes relevant context to collaborators. Do not put credentials into briefs. Keep private project information out of public issue reports. Publishing, paid services and other consequential actions remain subject to your actual instructions and host permissions.
